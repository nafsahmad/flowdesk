# Incident: HubSpot key rejected by lead-intake (drill)

**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Type:** Planned drill, simulating an expired or wrong credential

**What I noticed, and how:** Email alert "ALARM: flowdesk-lead-intake-errors" at 17:38 (about 6 minutes after the failure).

**Impact:** One test lead (drill.alarm@example.com) didn't reach HubSpot for about 10 minutes.

**Where I looked:**
1. The alert email named the Lambda.
2. CloudWatch logs for flowdesk-lead-intake: a "received" line for the submission, then a traceback ending in "HubSpot returned 401".
3. 401 means the key was rejected, so I checked the HUBSPOT_KEY environment variable.

**Cause:** HUBSPOT_KEY on flowdesk-lead-intake was wrong (one character changed on purpose).

**Fix:** Restored the correct key at 18:10.

**How I verified the fix:** Tally's retry delivered the lead at 18:12. Drill Alarm appeared in HubSpot with fd_utm_source=drill, fd_utm_campaign=alarm-test and fd_utm_content=A. The alarm returned to OK and the OK email arrived.

**How to stop it happening again:**
- The daily credential check (next step) will catch a bad or expired key before a real lead hits it.
- Keys are listed with rotation dates in the credentials table in decisions.md.
- Raising an error on HubSpot failures (instead of replying 200) is what made Tally retry, so no lead was lost.
