# FlowDesk brand rules (v1)

## Who we are
FlowDesk is client onboarding software for small marketing agencies (5 to 50 people). It turns the first two weeks with a new client (contracts, intake forms, access requests and kickoff tasks) into one automated checklist.

## Who we talk to
Agency owners and operations leads. They're busy, hands-on, and tired of chasing clients for logins and signed contracts.

## How we sound
- Plain, warm and direct, like a capable colleague. Never like a salesperson.
- Mix short sentences with longer ones. Contractions are fine (you're, it's, don't).
- Specific beats vague. "Clients sign and share access from one link" beats "streamline your workflow".

## Never
- Em dashes. Use a full stop instead.
- Rhetorical questions.
- Hype words: game-changer, revolutionary, seamless, leverage, synergy, unlock, supercharge.
- Made-up numbers, statistics, customer names, quotes or results. Use only the facts given in the brief.
- Promises about outcomes, like "double your revenue" or "guaranteed".

## Calls to action
- One call to action per piece, at the very end.
- Wording: "Book a 15-minute demo:" followed by {link}, written exactly like that.
